# GaiaGIS v5.12.0 — Field Script Recovery & Provenance Consistency

## What's New

- Recover verified eight-byte `CMUSC` static instruction decoding for the validated original English source, restoring related static CFG coverage in 24 Field scenes while preserving instruction boundaries and Actor/Slot/Entry identities. Decoder identity is explicit across old and new decoding rules.
- Expand English evidence to 14,030 reachable MESSAGE/ASK references and 13,267 Gameplay records. Restore three MAPJUMP source references and add Field Battle to Formation 652 evidence links. Existing reward records do not establish new chests, collection state or spatial coordinates.
- Add Context v6 and explicit decoder identity across Field Context, Interaction, Script Evidence, Gameplay and Dialogue validation. Reject mixed old/new CFG assets and support existing legacy Workspaces alongside the complete current English asset set.
- Provide controlled incremental migration with backup, journal and recovery support. Preserve source navigation across Field Canvas, Inspector, Walkmesh, Gateway, Entrance, Arrival and Access evidence.

## Compatibility

- Original Walkmesh and Field Visual geometry and bytes are unchanged. Restored MAPJUMP references do not become spatial Gateways automatically.
- Private Workspace assets are generated and used locally; the public Release and Pages do not contain original game data, dialogue text or private recovery files.

## Known Limitations

- Restored static CFG coverage does not verify original runtime execution. The Viewer does not infer story conditions, player choices, dialogue playback or reward collection.
- No fictional NPC, dialogue, battle or chest coordinates are generated.
- The eight-byte recovery applies to the validated source only; it does not establish compatibility with all languages, mods or platforms.
