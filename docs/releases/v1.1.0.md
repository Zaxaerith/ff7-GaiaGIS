# GaiaGIS v1.1.0 — Locations and entrances

- Locally generated WM0 named locations and field entrance records with
  triangle, world-script, FIELD.TBL and maplist provenance.
- Optional `gaia-poi.json`; the original two-file V1 loader remains usable.
- Markers, major-location labels, categories, name/alias/field search,
  keyboard navigation, fly-to, multiple-entrance inspector and deep links.
- Globe, Equirectangular, Mercator, Mollweide and Orthographic support,
  including synchronized projection morphing and reduced motion.
- Desktop and 390px/320px touch validation.

The installed validation dataset resolves 34 locations and 43 entrances.
Navigation points are derived from actual source trigger triangles;
availability is unknown. Gold Saucer and Northern Cave remain unresolved
in the static extractor. No manually estimated coordinates are included.

V1 geometry, parameterization and projection definitions are unchanged.
Climate V2/V2.1/V2.2 remain Experimental / Inconclusive.

This remains a code-only release: no proprietary game files, complete
derived geometry or POI coordinate dataset is distributed. Users generate
and load data from their own installation. GPL-3.0-only applies to
GaiaGIS original code; third-party and FF7 rights remain separate.

See [POI data and limitations](../v1.1/poi-data.md) and
[validation](../v1.1/validation.md).
