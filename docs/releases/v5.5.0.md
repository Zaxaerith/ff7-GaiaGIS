# GaiaGIS v5.5.0 — Regional Terrain Statistics

## What's New

- Add Global Overview, By Region and By Terrain views to Analysis, with bidirectional Region × Terrain counts and raw-area contributions, explicit percentage denominators, sorting, pagination and source-face navigation.
- Add per-terrain raw Y ranges, zero-horizontal-area and duplicate-source-face counts, area-weighted mean slope, seven slope-area bands, eight aspect directions and defined-aspect area coverage. Flat and undefined aspects remain separate rather than being averaged as angles.
- Verify the statistics against the original WM0 BASE source: 142,586 faces, 18 original Region IDs, 29 original Terrain IDs and 161 nonempty Region × Terrain cells. The complete cross-tabulation was checked against the independent research results.

## Verified Source Statistics

- Total raw source-area contribution: **67,692,515,599 raw²**.
- Zero-horizontal-area faces: **246**.
- Members of duplicate-geometry groups: **168**.
- Global area-weighted raw slope: approximately **5.036422°**.
- Defined-aspect area coverage: **21.8968%**.

The area value is the sum of original triangle X/Z projected-area contributions. It is not deduplicated coverage or km². Raw Y is not physically calibrated elevation, and raw-coordinate slope is not real-world planetary terrain slope.

## Compatibility

- Preserve original Region and Terrain IDs; do not apply encounter-region clamping or Terrain aliases to source groupings.
- Keep spherical Region navigation centers unchanged and preserve Topology, Routing, Field, Save, Native, World Variants and all 13 projections.
- No Workspace Manifest, Routing CSR or GeoPackage format changes and no new mandatory data assets.

## Known Limitations

- Region and Terrain values are original game attributes, not automatically ecological classes or administrative boundaries.
- Area contributions do not represent unique coverage. Regional connectivity, polygon union and hydrological analysis are not implemented.
- Source-coordinate metrics do not establish physical elevation, distance or slope.

Users must provide data from a legally owned Final Fantasy VII installation. GaiaGIS does not distribute original game files or private Workspace data.
